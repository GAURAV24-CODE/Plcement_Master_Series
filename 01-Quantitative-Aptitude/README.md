## 📌 About This Section

Welcome to the **Quantitative Aptitude** section of my Placement
Master Series.

This section focuses on developing mathematical problem-solving
skills, numerical reasoning, calculation speed, and accuracy for
campus placement examinations and competitive recruitment tests.

The materials are organized topic-wise, with concept explanations,
important formulas, solved examples, shortcuts, and practice
questions.

The goal is to understand the logic behind every question and
develop the ability to solve problems accurately under time limits.

## 🎯 Learning Objectives

- Strengthen fundamental mathematical concepts.
- Master important formulas and calculation techniques.
- Improve numerical reasoning and analytical thinking.
- Learn time-saving methods and shortcuts.
- Solve easy, intermediate, and placement-level questions.
- Improve accuracy and speed through regular practice.
- Prepare for aptitude assessments conducted by recruiters.
