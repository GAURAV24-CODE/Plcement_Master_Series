DAY 02 — HCF & LCM → PRIME FACTORIZATION → NUMBER OF FACTORS

HCF

HCF = Highest Common Factor of given numbers.

Example:

Factors of 12 = 1, 2, 3, 4, 6, 12
Factors of 18 = 1, 2, 3, 6, 9, 18

HCF = 6

LCM

LCM = Least Common Multiple of given numbers.

Example:

Multiples of 4 = 4, 8, 12, 16, 20...
Multiples of 6 = 6, 12, 18, 24...

LCM = 12

PRIME FACTORIZATION

Prime Factorization = Writing a number as a product of prime numbers.

Example:

60 = 2 × 2 × 3 × 5

Therefore:

60 = 2² × 3 × 5

HCF USING PRIME FACTORIZATION

Take the common prime factors
with the smallest powers.

Example:

12 = 2² × 3
18 = 2 × 3²

HCF = 2¹ × 3¹
= 6

LCM USING PRIME FACTORIZATION

Take all prime factors
with the highest powers.

Example:

12 = 2² × 3
18 = 2 × 3²

LCM = 2² × 3²
= 36

IMPORTANT RELATION

For two positive numbers:

HCF × LCM = Product of the two numbers

Example:

12 × 18 = 216

HCF = 6
LCM = 36

6 × 36 = 216

NUMBER OF FACTORS

If:

N = aᵖ × bᑫ × cʳ

Number of factors:

(p+1)(q+1)(r+1)

EXAMPLE

36 = 2² × 3²

Number of factors:

(2+1)(2+1)
= 3 × 3
= 9

FACTORS OF 36

1, 2, 3, 4, 6, 9, 12, 18, 36

Total = 9

QUICK METHOD

HCF:
Smallest powers of common primes.

LCM:
Highest powers of all primes.

Number of factors:
Add 1 to every power and multiply.

IMPORTANT

HCF is always ≤ each number.

LCM is always ≥ each number.

For two numbers:
HCF × LCM = Product of numbers.
