DAY 01 — NUMBER BASICS → FACTORS & MULTIPLES → DIVISIBILITY

NUMBER BASICS

Number = A value used for counting or calculation.

TYPES OF NUMBERS

Natural Numbers = 1, 2, 3, 4, ...

Whole Numbers = 0, 1, 2, 3, ...

Integers = ..., -3, -2, -1, 0, 1, 2, 3, ...

Even Numbers = Divisible by 2
Example: 2, 4, 6, 8

Odd Numbers = Not divisible by 2
Example: 1, 3, 5, 7

Prime Number = Has exactly 2 factors: 1 and itself.
Example: 2, 3, 5, 7

Composite Number = Has more than 2 factors.
Example: 4, 6, 8, 9

FACTORS

Factor = A number that divides another number exactly.

Example:

Factors of 12:
1, 2, 3, 4, 6, 12

Important:
1 is a factor of every positive number.

MULTIPLES

Multiple = Result obtained by multiplying a number by 1, 2, 3,...

Example:

Multiples of 5:
5, 10, 15, 20, 25,...

FACTOR vs MULTIPLE

Factor → Divides exactly
Multiple → Obtained by multiplication

Example:
3 is a factor of 12.
12 is a multiple of 3.

DIVISIBILITY RULES

2 → Last digit is even.

3 → Sum of digits divisible by 3.

4 → Last 2 digits divisible by 4.

5 → Last digit = 0 or 5.

6 → Divisible by both 2 and 3.

8 → Last 3 digits divisible by 8.

9 → Sum of digits divisible by 9.

10 → Last digit = 0.

11 → Difference between alternating digit sums
is 0 or divisible by 11.

EXAMPLES

246 → Divisible by 2

123 → 1+2+3 = 6
Therefore divisible by 3.

125 → Last digit 5
Therefore divisible by 5.

324 → Last 2 digits = 24
24 is divisible by 4.

QUICK METHOD

1. Check the last digit.
2. Check digit sum.
3. Check last 2 digits.
4. Check last 3 digits.
5. Apply the correct divisibility rule.

IMPORTANT

2, 5, 10 → Check last digit.

3, 9 → Check digit sum.

4 → Check last 2 digits.

8 → Check last 3 digits.

6 → Check both 2 and 3.
