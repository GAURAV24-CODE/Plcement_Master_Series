DAY 13 — CLOCKS & CALENDARS

CLOCKS

Clock = Questions based on time, angles and positions of clock hands.


IMPORTANT

Minute hand moves faster than hour hand.

Minute hand:
6° per minute

Hour hand:
0.5° per minute


ANGLE FORMULA

Angle = |30H - 5.5M|

H = Hour
M = Minutes


EXAMPLE

At 3:00:

Angle = |30×3 - 5.5×0|
      = 90°

Answer = 90°


CALENDARS

Calendar = Questions based on days, dates and leap years.


IMPORTANT

Normal year = 365 days
= 52 weeks + 1 day

Leap year = 366 days
= 52 weeks + 2 days


LEAP YEAR RULE

Divisible by 4 = Usually leap year.

Century year must be divisible by 400.

Example:
2000 = Leap year
1900 = Not leap year


QUICK METHOD

For clocks:
Use angle formula.

For calendars:
Check leap year + odd days.


IMPORTANT

Always check whether the year is
normal or leap year.
