DAY 08 — STATEMENT & ASSUMPTION

ASSUMPTION

Assumption = An unstated idea that must be accepted for the statement to make sense.


EXAMPLE

Statement:
"Buy this phone because it has a long battery life."

Assumption:
People want phones with long battery life.

Answer = Valid assumption


HOW TO IDENTIFY

Ask:

"Is this idea necessary for the statement?"


QUICK METHOD

1. Read the statement.
2. Read the assumption.
3. Check whether the statement depends on it.
4. If necessary = Assumption is implicit.


IMPORTANT

Assumption is NOT directly stated.
It is an idea behind the statement.
