DAY 05 — DIRECTIONS & RANKING

DIRECTIONS

Direction = Finding the position or movement of a person/object.


MAIN DIRECTIONS

        North
          ↑
West ←         → East
          ↓
        South


DIAGONALS

North-East = NE
North-West = NW
South-East = SE
South-West = SW


EXAMPLE

A walks 5 km North.
Then 3 km East.

Final direction from starting point = North-East.


OPPOSITE DIRECTIONS

North ↔ South
East ↔ West
NE ↔ SW
NW ↔ SE


RANKING

Ranking = Finding a person's position in a line or group.


IMPORTANT FORMULAS

Rank from Top + Rank from Bottom - 1
= Total number of people


Example:

Rank from top = 8
Rank from bottom = 13

Total = 8 + 13 - 1
     = 20


FIND OPPOSITE RANK

Total - Given Rank + 1


QUICK METHOD

1. Draw directions.
2. Mark starting point.
3. Follow every movement.
4. For ranking, identify Top/Bottom or Left/Right.
5. Use the formula.


IMPORTANT

Always draw a small diagram for direction questions.
