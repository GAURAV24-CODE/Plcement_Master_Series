DAY 09 — DATA SUFFICIENCY

DATA SUFFICIENCY

Data Sufficiency = Checking whether the given information is enough to answer a question.


TYPICAL FORMAT

Question:
What is the value of X?

Statement I
Statement II


POSSIBILITIES

1. Statement I alone is sufficient.
2. Statement II alone is sufficient.
3. Both together are sufficient.
4. Either alone is sufficient.
5. Even both are insufficient.


EXAMPLE

What is X?

I. X + 5 = 10
II. X is a positive number

Statement I alone:
X = 5

Therefore I is sufficient.


QUICK METHOD

1. Check Statement I alone.
2. Check Statement II alone.
3. Check both together.
4. Do not solve more than necessary.


IMPORTANT

Data Sufficiency asks:
"Is the information enough?"

It does not always ask you to find the answer.
