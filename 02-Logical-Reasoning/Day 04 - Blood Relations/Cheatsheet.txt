DAY 04 — BLOOD RELATIONS

BASIC RELATIONS

Father's father = Grandfather
Father's mother = Grandmother

Mother's father = Maternal Grandfather
Mother's mother = Maternal Grandmother

Father's brother = Uncle
Mother's brother = Uncle

Father's sister = Aunt
Mother's sister = Aunt

Brother's son = Nephew
Brother's daughter = Niece

Sister's son = Nephew
Sister's daughter = Niece


GENDER

Father = Male
Brother = Male
Son = Male
Uncle = Male
Husband = Male

Mother = Female
Sister = Female
Daughter = Female
Aunt = Female
Wife = Female


GENERATION LEVEL

+2 = Grandparents
+1 = Parents / Uncle / Aunt
 0 = Self / Brother / Sister / Cousin
-1 = Children
-2 = Grandchildren


EXAMPLE

A is the brother of B.
B is the mother of C.

A's relation to C?

B = Mother of C
A = Brother of B

Therefore:
A = Uncle of C


QUICK METHOD

1. Identify the gender.
2. Identify the generation.
3. Start from the person mentioned.
4. Draw a small family tree.
5. Follow the relationship step by step.
6. Check the final relationship.


IMPORTANT

Do not solve long blood-relation questions mentally.
Draw a simple family tree.
