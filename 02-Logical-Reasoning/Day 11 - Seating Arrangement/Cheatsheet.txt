DAY 11 — SEATING ARRANGEMENT

SEATING ARRANGEMENT

Seating Arrangement = Finding the positions of people according to given conditions.


TYPES

- Linear arrangement
- Circular arrangement
- Facing North/South
- Facing Center/Outside


LINEAR

A B C D E

If A is left of B:
A ← B


CIRCULAR

People sit around a circle.

Facing Center:
Left = Clockwise
Right = Anti-clockwise


IMPORTANT RULE

For circular arrangement:
Always identify the facing direction first.


QUICK METHOD

1. Draw the seats.
2. Place fixed positions first.
3. Place definite relationships.
4. Use remaining clues.
5. Check all conditions.


COMMON WORDS

Immediate left = Next position
Immediate right = Next position
Between = In the middle
Adjacent = Next to
Opposite = Directly across


IMPORTANT

Do not try to remember the whole arrangement mentally.
Draw it.
