DAY 12 — PUZZLES

PUZZLES

Puzzle = A set of clues used to determine relationships, positions or arrangements.


COMMON TYPES

- Floor puzzle
- Box puzzle
- Person arrangement
- Age puzzle
- Scheduling puzzle
- Distribution puzzle
- Ranking puzzle


EXAMPLE

A lives above B.
C lives below B.

Order:

A
B
C


QUICK METHOD

1. Read all clues.
2. Identify fixed information.
3. Make a table/diagram.
4. Place definite information first.
5. Use remaining clues.
6. Verify every condition.


IMPORTANT

Use elimination when possible.

Do not solve everything mentally.
Use tables and diagrams.
