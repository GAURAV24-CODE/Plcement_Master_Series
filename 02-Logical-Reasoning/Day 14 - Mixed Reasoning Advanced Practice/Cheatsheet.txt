DAY 14 — MIXED REASONING & ADVANCED PRACTICE

PURPOSE

Combine all previous reasoning topics
and solve mixed placement-level questions.


TOPICS TO MIX

- Analogy
- Classification
- Number Series
- Alphabet Series
- Coding-Decoding
- Blood Relations
- Directions
- Ranking
- Syllogism
- Statement & Conclusion
- Statement & Assumption
- Data Sufficiency
- Cause & Effect
- Seating Arrangement
- Puzzles
- Clocks & Calendars


QUICK METHOD

1. Identify the question type.
2. Select the correct method.
3. Solve using shortcut.
4. Check the answer.
5. Track the time taken.


PLACEMENT FOCUS

Accuracy + Speed

Do not spend too much time
on one difficult question.


IMPORTANT

Mixed practice is important because
actual placement tests do not always
group questions topic-wise.
