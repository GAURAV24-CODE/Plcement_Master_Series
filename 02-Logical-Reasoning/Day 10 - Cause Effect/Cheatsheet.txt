DAY 10 — CAUSE & EFFECT

CAUSE & EFFECT

Cause = Reason why something happens.
Effect = Result produced by something.


EXAMPLE

Heavy rain occurred.
Roads became flooded.

Cause = Heavy rain
Effect = Flooding


HOW TO IDENTIFY

Cause usually happens BEFORE the effect.


EXAMPLE

Statement I:
There was heavy rainfall.

Statement II:
Several roads were flooded.

Possible relationship:
Rainfall → Flooding


QUICK METHOD

1. Identify the event.
2. Ask "Why did it happen?"
3. Identify the reason = Cause.
4. Identify the result = Effect.
5. Check whether the relationship is logical.


IMPORTANT

Do not assume two events are related
just because they happened together.
