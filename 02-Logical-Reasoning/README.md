## 🧠 Learning Methodology

Each topic follows a structured approach to help build conceptual
clarity and problem-solving ability.

### 📖 Learning Flow

**Concept → Explanation → Solved Examples → Shortcuts → Practice → Analysis → Revision**

### Step 1 — Understand the Concept
Learn the basic rules, patterns, and principles behind the topic.

### Step 2 — Observe the Pattern
Identify relationships, sequences, conditions, and logical clues.

### Step 3 — Study Solved Examples
Understand the reasoning process used to reach the answer.

### Step 4 — Learn Shortcuts
Explore time-saving techniques where applicable.

### Step 5 — Practise Independently
Solve questions without immediately checking the answers.

### Step 6 — Analyse Mistakes
Identify incorrect assumptions, missed patterns, and calculation
errors.

### Step 7 — Revise Regularly
Revisit difficult questions and practise similar problems.

## ⚡ Important Reasoning Strategies

### 1. Identify the Pattern First
For series and sequence questions, check differences, multiplication,
division, alternating patterns, and position-based relationships.

### 2. Use Diagrams
For blood relations, directions, and seating arrangements, draw
simple diagrams instead of solving everything mentally.

### 3. Follow the Given Conditions
In puzzles and arrangements, write down every condition and eliminate
possibilities systematically.

### 4. Avoid Unsupported Assumptions
For syllogisms, statements, and conclusions, use only the information
provided in the question.

### 5. Manage Your Time
Do not spend too long on one difficult question. Practise recognizing
when to move forward and return later.

### 6. Review Every Incorrect Answer
Understand why your approach failed and record the correct reasoning.
